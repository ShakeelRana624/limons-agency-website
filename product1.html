Get in Touch

A better way for broadcasters
to successfully engage their audiences

through a fusion of social networks, gaming and TV content.

Telly connects live television with real-time audience participation, social conversation, premium content and centralized show management.

01Social TV

Live Quiz Challenge

A Player vs. Player experience synchronized with live TV, designed to turn passive viewing into competitive, rewarding participation.

Viewers compete head-to-head in synchronized quiz rounds, with scores and progress visible in real time.

Earned points can unlock tactical power-ups such as Instawin while the live game is still in progress.

Special abilities like Lights Out let players disrupt an opponent and add another strategic layer to the quiz.

02Real-Time Interactivity

Live Interactions & Voting

Polls, rewards and moderated conversation keep the audience active throughout the broadcast instead of watching from the sidelines.

Audience participation is rewarded instantly, turning each vote or interaction into a reason to keep engaging.

Live vote results surface audience sentiment as it develops, giving viewers immediate feedback on the wider conversation.

A moderated comment stream brings viewers into the same real-time social conversation around the show.

03Dedicated Media Hub

Shows & VIP Program Portals

Dedicated show hubs combine live schedules, shareable stories and premium content so each program can extend beyond the broadcast itself.

Browse show listings and immediately see what is live, what is coming next and when viewers should return.

News and show stories can be shared directly from the experience, extending the program into the viewer's social timeline.

Premium interviews, backstage media and VIP articles can be unlocked with participation points earned inside the app.

04Management Console

Centralized Show Dashboard

Production teams control shows, publishing and audience operations from one interface without needing a new app deployment for every update.

1

Create and configure shows, schedules, branding and publishing states from one console.

2

Manage users, notifications, breaking news and show operations from the same production workspace.

3

Adjust live experiences and content without forcing the audience through an app re-deployment.
